  The main Actors in Hospital Management System
No    Actor             Description

1,    Admin         Manage users,doctors,hospital information, and system operations.
2,    Cashier       Generate bills,processes payment, and prints receipts.
3,    Doctor        Views patient records,diagnoses patients,prescribes medicine, and updates medical records.
4,    Labratory     Views test requests,performs labratory tests, records results, and generates labratory reports
5,    Nurse         Views patient information,monitors patients, and manages patient care
6,    Patient        Requests appointments and views personal information and medical history
7,    Payment       Verifies and processes electronic payments and sends pyment status
8,    Pharmacist     Manages medicines, views prescriptions, dispenses medicine ,and updates medicine stock.
9,    Recieptionist    Register patients,books appointments,and updates patient information.
