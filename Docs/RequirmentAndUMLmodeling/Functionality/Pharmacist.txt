2.7 Pharmacist Functional Requirements

2. View Prescriptions
3. Dispense Medicine
4. Update Medicine Stock
5. Manage Medicine Inventory
6. Search Medicine

9. Generate Medicine Reports
10. Notify Admin About Low Medicine Stock
11. View Medicine History
12. Manage Medicine Categories
