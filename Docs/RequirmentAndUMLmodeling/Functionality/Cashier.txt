Functionalities

      The Cashier is supposed to handle all activities regarding patient billing and payment
      1.Cashier Account Login: The cashier can log in with a username and password to access billing and record patient payments.
      2. Generate bills:The Cashier will be able to generate bills for hospital services rendered to patients.

      3. View Patient Billing Information: The Cashier will be able to view patient billing information.

      4. Process Payments: The Cashier will be able to accept and process patient payments.

      5. Verify Payments: The Cashier will be able to verify patients' payment information.

      6. View Payment History: The Cashier will be able to view previous patient payment information.

      7. Print Receipts: The Cashier will be able to generate and print payment receipts.

      8. Update Payment Information: The Cashier will be able to update payment information when necessary.

      9. Manage Outstanding Bills: The Cashier will be able to view and manage unpaid patients bills.

10. Calculate Total Cost: The Cashier will be able to calculate the total cost of hospital services.

      11. Generate Payment Reports: The Cashier will be able to generate daily and monthly payment reports.



         User Stories

      1. As a Cashier, I would like to generate patient bills so that the patients can be aware of the cost of hospital services.

      2. As a Cashier, I would like to process patient payments so that patients can make their payments.

      3. As a Cashier, I would like to view patient billing information so that I can check their outstanding balance.

      4. As a Cashier, I would like to verify payment information so that I can be sure of its accuracy.

      5. As a Cashier, I would like to print payment receipts so that patients can receive proof of payment.

      6. As a Cashier, I would like to view payment history so that I can keep track of my previous transactions.

      7. As a Cashier, I would like to update payment information so that payment information will be accurate.

      8. As a Cashier, I would like to manage outstanding bills so that I can know the patients with unpaid bills.

      9. As a Cashier, I would like to calculate total hospital service costs so that patients can be charged accurately.
      
      10. As a Cashier, I would like to generate daily and monthly payment reports so that I can monitor hospital financial activities
